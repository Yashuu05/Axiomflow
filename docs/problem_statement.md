# **INTELLIGENT BUSINESS WORKFLOW AUTOMATION**

### Background

- Small and medium-sized businesses often depend on emails, spreadsheets, 
documents, messaging platforms, and disconnected software systems for daily operations. 
- Employees spend significant time searching for information, cross-checking 
records, tracking pending tasks, following up with stakeholders, and manually transferring information between systems. 
- Traditional automation generally relies on fixed rules, while real business workflows are often dynamic and may require information from multiple sources before deciding the
next action. 

---

### Challenge 

- Design an intelligent system capable of understanding a business objective, gathering relevant information, identifying the required actions, and assisting in executing the complete workflow with minimal manual effort. 
- The solution should handle multi-step workflows, rather than simply answering individual questions. 
- For example, a system may identify overdue financial obligations, retrieve relevant records, determine priority, recommend follow-up actions, generate communication, request approval where required, and track resolution. 

---

### Key Requirements 

The solution should demonstrate: 
- Understanding of a business objective 
- Information gathering from multiple sources 
- Multi-step planning and execution 
- Integration with documents, spreadsheets, databases, APIs, emails, or 
simulated systems 
- Ability to handle missing information or failed tasks 
- Dynamic re-planning when conditions change 
- Human approval or escalation for important actions 
- Basic audit trail of system actions 

---

### Expected Outcome 

- A working prototype demonstrating how a repetitive business workflow can be made faster,more reliable, traceable, and easier to manage.
